We rebuilt the <a href="/billionaire-database/">Billionaire database</a>.

The first version was a filterable sticker-book — cards, stats, receipts. The rebuild is about the people: stubs for ~3400 and some info on ~600 billionaires. There is still a lot of work to do, but it's a start.

What's new:

- **Person pages** — family, co-founders, early backers, board seats.
- **Group pages** — the larger families and founding teams have a page each.
- **Company pages** — 337 companies (PayPal, Tesla, Walmart, Meta, Disney…), listing founders, heirs, shareholders and board members, and links to groups.
- **Company name histories** — renames and takeovers.
- **Research briefs for gaps** — <a href="/billionaire-database/gaps/">where we still need data</a>

Data behind it: 3,394 people, 1,203 person-to-person records, company memberships, a company-names wherever we could match them up.

Most of the research so far came through google and ChatGPT, and we're maybe a fifth populated — possibly optimistic. Pull down a research brief and help us find more coverage please.
