Co-founders

They built it together

Elon Musk built Zip2 with his brother and PayPal alongside Peter Thiel. Larry Page and Sergey Brin built Google together; Thomas Secunda and Charles Zegar built Bloomberg with Michael Bloomberg. Most of the top fifty got there in pairs or teams — the PayPal and Google founding groups alone account for a dozen of them.

Early investors

The first money in

Stakes bought when the companies were worth almost nothing — what was paid, and what it became:

  • Bechtolsheim → Page · Brin — wrote Google its first cheque: $100,000 in 1998, before the company existed. It made him a billionaire.
  • Thiel → Zuckerberg — $500,000 for 10% of Facebook in 2004, the first outside money Zuckerberg took — and a board seat he kept for 18 years.
  • Son → Ma — $20 million into Alibaba in 2000, decided after one meeting. It returned tens of billions.
  • Buffett → Wang Chuanfu — $230 million for 10% of BYD in 2008. Munger called Wang "a combination of Edison and Jack Welch".

Board seats

Still on the board

Board seats held by people on this list — the seat usually came with an investment or a family stake:

  • Ellison → Musk — joined Tesla's board in 2018, calling himself Musk's close friend — then put about $1 billion into the Twitter takeover.
  • Doerr · Moritz → Page · Brin — Kleiner Perkins and Sequoia paid roughly $12.5m each for Google in 1999; the board seats came with the money.
  • Kimbal Musk — on Tesla's board since 2004: the family keeps a second vote inside the company.
